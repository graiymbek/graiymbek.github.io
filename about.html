I am a Data Analyst with a strong foundation in analytical problem-solving, business intelligence, and data-driven decision-making. My career began in scientific research, where years of designing experiments, analyzing complex datasets, and publishing research in high-impact scientific journals developed a rigorous analytical mindset, exceptional attention to detail, and a systematic approach to solving complex problems.

Motivated by my passion for working with data, I transitioned into the field of data analytics, where I combine technical expertise with business insight to transform raw, complex data into meaningful, actionable insights. I specialize in SQL, Power BI, Excel, Python, and data modeling, building interactive dashboards and automated reporting solutions that help organizations make informed decisions.

I am a Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate (PL-300) and have experience developing enterprise Business Intelligence solutions across healthcare, supply chain, retail, and business operations. My work includes designing SQL databases, creating executive Power BI dashboards, developing KPI reporting, and delivering data visualization solutions that improve operational efficiency and support strategic decision-making.

I believe every dataset tells a story. My goal is to uncover that story, communicate it clearly through intuitive visualizations, and provide insights that enable organizations to make smarter, data-driven decisions.
