About me

From the bench to the dashboard.

Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate (PL-300) with a research background in biology, I turn complex, messy datasets into dashboards that drive real decisions.

Every career teaches you how to solve problems. Mine began in scientific research, where years of designing experiments, analyzing complex biological data, and publishing research in high-impact journals taught me how to ask the right questions, validate evidence, and uncover meaningful patterns hidden within complex datasets. Those same analytical skills naturally led me into data analytics. Today I design SQL databases, build Power BI dashboards, and transform raw business data into clear, actionable insights that support smarter decision-making. Whether analyzing healthcare, finance, retail, or supply chain data, I enjoy turning messy information into intuitive dashboards that help organizations understand their business and make confident decisions. For me, data analytics isn't just about creating charts, it's about solving real business problems through thoughtful analysis, clean data models, and meaningful storytelling .
